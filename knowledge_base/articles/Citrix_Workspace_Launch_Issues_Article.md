# Citrix Workspace Launch Issues

## Symptoms
* Citrix closes immediately after launch.
* Application does not open.
* Black screen after login.
* Citrix session freezes.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Clear Citrix Workspace cache.
2. Restart Citrix Workspace.
3. Verify internet connection.
4. Reinstall Citrix Workspace.
5. Check VDI access permissions.
6. Restart the computer.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate if the application still fails after reinstalling Citrix Workspace.
