# Microsoft Teams Audio Issues

## Symptoms

- No sound in meetings.
- Microphone not detected.
- Participants cannot hear user.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Verify audio device selection.
2. Run Teams device test.
3. Reconnect headset.
4. Restart Teams.
5. Update audio drivers.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate if audio devices work in other applications but fail in Teams.
